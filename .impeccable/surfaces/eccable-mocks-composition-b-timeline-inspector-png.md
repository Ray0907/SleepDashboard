## Scope and mode

Home/Overview dashboard mockup for the native macOS app. Mode: Operate.

## Audience, job, and task

An individual imports AutoSleep CSV data and needs to compare longer-term
patterns while retaining the ability to inspect one exact night.

## Chosen direction

Apple-native Timeline + Inspector. Approved comp:
`.impeccable/mocks/composition-b-timeline-inspector.png`.

The memorable interaction is one selected-date rule crossing all six aligned
metric tracks while a native inspector shows the exact values for that night.

## Composition and component grammar

- Native macOS split view with system-material sidebar and integrated toolbar.
- Unboxed summary metrics form the top rhythm; no score or gamification.
- Six charts share one date axis inside a continuous analysis canvas rather
  than a grid of equal cards.
- A quiet inspector uses definition rows and separators, not nested cards.
- System typography, 12px grouped-surface radii, hairline separators, minimal
  elevation, functional translucency only in structural chrome.
- Semantic chart identities: indigo duration, navy bedtime, violet deep sleep,
  blue efficiency, coral BPM, teal HRV.

## Constraints

- v1 has no AI panel; AI Insights remain a future optional capability.
- All displayed values are labelled sample data until real imports exist.
- Light and dark appearances keep identical hierarchy and chart identities.
- Preserve exact units, missing-value gaps, pointer selection, keyboard focus,
  VoiceOver labelling, and reduced-transparency alternatives.
